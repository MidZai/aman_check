AMAN CHECK - HOW TO INSTALL
===========================

1. Drag "Aman Check" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open Aman Check from your Applications folder.


THE FIRST TIME, macOS BLOCKS THE APP
------------------------------------

Aman Check is free and open source, but it isn't signed with a paid
Apple Developer ID, so macOS can't verify who made it and blocks it
the first time you open it. You only need to allow it once.


macOS 15 Sequoia or later

  1. Open Aman Check, then click "Done" in the warning.
  2. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down
     to "Security".
  3. Next to "Aman Check was blocked to protect your Mac",
     click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

  If the "Open Anyway" button isn't there, open Aman Check once more,
  then go back to System Settings.


macOS 14 Sonoma

  In the Applications folder, Control-click Aman Check, choose "Open",
  then click "Open".


Or, in Terminal (any version)

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Aman Check.app"


After that, Aman Check opens normally, like any other app.
